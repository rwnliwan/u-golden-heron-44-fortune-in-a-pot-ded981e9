A single line, at most 120 characters, telling a houseplant's fortune: kind, funny, unmistakably about the plant on the windowsill, for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9).

## Entry

> You will outlive three of your keeper's resolutions — and be watered by the fourth.

Run with Node 22: `node main.ts` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT`).

## Craft

Prediction cadence ("You will..."), kind and funny, written for this plant and its keeper. Saturated angles avoided: "soon you will outgrow this pot", "someone will finally learn your name this spring", sunbeam, spring, "you chose this window on purpose", "big leaf energy", "a sunny Tuesday is coming".
